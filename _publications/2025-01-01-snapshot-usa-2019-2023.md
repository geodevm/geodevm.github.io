SNAPSHOT USA is a coordinated camera-trap survey run each fall by collaborators across all 50 states. This data paper describes the first five years of the survey. I contributed camera-trap data from the Twin Cities.

The dataset is openly available on [Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.k0p2ngfhn).
